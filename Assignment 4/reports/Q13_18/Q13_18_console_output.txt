==============================================================================
Problem 13.18 - 1,3-Butadiene from 1-butene with steam, 950 K, 1 bar
==============================================================================
Parameter source: Smith, Van Ness & Abbott, 6th ed. (Antoine constants, molar volumes, Wilson and NRTL parameters as supplied by the student)

--- Problem statement ---
1,3-Butadiene is produced by the dehydrogenation of 1-butene: C2H5CH:CH2(g) -> CH2:CHCH:CH2(g) + H2(g). Side reactions are suppressed by steam. If equilibrium is attained at 950 K and 1 bar and the reactor product contains 10 mol-% 1,3-butadiene, find (a) the mole fractions of the other species in the product gas, (b) the mole fraction of steam required in the feed.

--- Governing equations ---
    Reaction: 1-butene -> 1,3-butadiene + H2,  nu = (-1, +1, +1);  steam is inert
    K = exp(-dG0(T)/RT) from Eq. (13.18);   ideal gas:  K = y_B y_H2 (P/P0) / y_C4

--- Given data ---
  T = 950 K
  P = 1 bar
  y_butadiene in product = 0.1 

--- Step 1 - equilibrium constant at 950 K ---
  species | nu_i | A | 10^3 B | 10^6 C | 10^-5 D | dHf298 [J/mol] | dGf298 [J/mol]
  1-butene | -1 | 1.967 | 31.63 | -9.873 | 0 | -540 | 70340
  butadiene | 1 | 2.734 | 26.786 | -8.882 | 0 | 109240 | 149795
  H2 | 1 | 3.249 | 0.422 | 0 | 0.083 | 0 | 0
  dA, dB, dC, dD = 4.016, -0.004422, 9.91e-07, 8300 
  dH298 = sum nu_i dHf_i = 109.78 kJ/mol
  dG298 = sum nu_i dGf_i = 79.455 kJ/mol
  K(298.15 K) = exp(-dG298/RT0) = 1.20036e-14 
  dH(950.0 K) = 119.029 kJ/mol
  ln K(950.0 K) = -0.619859 
  K(950.0 K) = 0.53802 

--- Step 2 - part (a): product composition ---
    Steam is inert, so butadiene and H2 are formed in equal amounts:  y_H2 = y_B = 0.10
    K = y_B y_H2 P / y_C4   =>   y_C4 = y_B^2 P / K,      y_steam = 1 - y_B - y_H2 - y_C4
  y_1-butene = 0.0185867 
  y_steam = 0.781413 

--- Step 3 - part (b): steam in the feed (basis 1 mol 1-butene fed, e mol reacted, s mol steam) ---
    Product: butene (1-e), butadiene e, H2 e, steam s;  N = 1 + s + e
    y_B / y_C4 = e / (1-e)  =>  e = y_B/(y_B + y_C4);   N = e / y_B;   s = N - 1 - e;   feed fraction = s/(1+s)
  extent e = 0.843265 mol
  total product moles N = 8.43265 mol
  steam fed s = 6.58939 mol per mol butene

--- Final answers ---
  [RESULT a] y 1,3-butadiene (given) = 0.1 -
  [RESULT a] y hydrogen = 0.1 -
  [RESULT a] y 1-butene = 0.0185867 -
  [RESULT a] y steam = 0.781413 -
  [RESULT b] steam fed per mol 1-butene = 6.58939 mol/mol
  [RESULT b] mole fraction of steam in feed = 0.868237 -
